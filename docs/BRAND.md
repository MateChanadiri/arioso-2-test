# Arioso — brand notes

**Name:** Arioso — the musical term for a melody that is half aria, half speech: personal, expressive, yours.
Three syllables (ar-ee-OH-so), easy to say, reads like a real music brand, and works as one word on an icon.

**Tagline:** *Music in your own voice.*

**Alternatives considered:** Cadenza, Sonore, Vesper, Lumen, Coda, Mélo, Halcyon, Tessera.
Arioso won on meaning (personalization + melody), pronounceability and distinctiveness.

**Visual identity:** warm, confident, slightly editorial. Deep violet → rose gradients, one bright accent,
generous rounded shapes, no skeuomorphism.

**App icon:** four rounded "voice bars" of different heights (a melody contour, also a tiny equaliser) in white on
a solid colour field — see `Arioso/AppIcon*.icon`. The splash animation stamps the same four bars one by one.

**Wordmark:** the name set in a rounded humanist sans, medium weight, tight tracking, with the four-bar mark as
a stand-in for the leading "A".

**Not verified:** I could not check App Store, domain or trademark databases from the build environment, so
availability of the name is **unverified**. Before publishing publicly, search the App Store, the USPTO / EUIPO
trademark databases and domain registrars. The name lives in `Config/Brand.xcconfig` and is easy to change.
